Report only evidence supplied by the traffic prediction capability:
- timestamp
- Enterprise predicted traffic
- RAN predicted traffic
- PON predicted traffic

"At 21:15, predicted offered traffic is 88.50 Gbps total: Enterprise 4.66 Gbps, RAN 45.17 Gbps, and PON 38.66 Gbps."
